History of Melon beauty salon.

Melon Beauty Salon was established in 1989, originally opening in a modest storefront at 682 Hougang Ave 8. It later expanded to Hougang Central Block 811, #01-192. During construction of our Star of Kovan outlet, we temporarily relocated to 1187 Upper Serangoon Rd, #01-52. By 2021, we had moved to our permanent location at Star of Kovan. Throughout the years, our core mission has remained consistent: to offer a one-stop service in a welcoming and comfortable environment where our customers can be pampered and well taken care of.
